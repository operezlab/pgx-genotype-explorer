How to read the tables
  1. The *1 (reference) row shows the most common letter at each position.
  2. The Your data row shows your two letters (one from each parent) at the same positions.
  3. Each star-allele row shows only the letter(s) that define that allele. A blank cell means it has the same letter as *1.
  4. Wherever your letters differ from *1, find the allele row that has that letter. One copy of the different letter means you carry one copy of that allele; two copies means both of your chromosomes carry it.
  5. Write your genotype as two alleles (e.g. *1/*2) and use Check your work.
